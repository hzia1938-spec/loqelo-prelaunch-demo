What is Loqelo?+

Loqelo is an upcoming platform connecting people with service providers for the home, garden and office. Describe what you need, receive offers and choose a provider.

When will Loqelo launch?+

Our planned launch is February 2027. Join the waitlist to hear about launch news and availability in your country.

Can I join as a service provider?+

Absolutely. Whether you work independently or run a business, choose “I provide services” in the sign-up form and tell us where you work.

Is this the live Loqelo service?+

This is a pre-launch website demo. The platform screens show the planned experience. Demo form entries stay in your browser and are not sent to a live mailing list.

Your privacy, explained.

This public concept demo saves form entries in this browser’s session storage only. No sign-up email is sent and no marketing account receives your details. Entries disappear when this tab’s browser session ends. You can export or delete your local demo entries below.

Analytics are disabled in this demo. Cookie preferences are stored locally. A live deployment needs its own privacy notice, mailing-list service and consent setup before collecting real subscriptions.
