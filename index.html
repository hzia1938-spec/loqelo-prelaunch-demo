    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Short answers to the questions we hear most.

    When does Loqelo launch?

    We’re planning to launch in February 2027. Join the waitlist and we’ll let you know as soon as Loqelo opens in your country.

    Does it cost anything to join the waitlist?

    No. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.

    I’m a service provider. Can I join?

    Yes. Choose “I provide services”, tell us the country where you work, and we’ll keep you posted about provider sign-up.

    Which countries will Loqelo be available in?

    We’re collecting interest from many countries. Your sign-up helps us decide where to launch first.

    What happens with my email address?

    We use it only to send you news about the Loqelo launch. You can unsubscribe at any time. We never share it with analytics tools or third parties.

    Which languages does Loqelo speak?

    The site picks your language automatically. You can switch between English, Polish and German at any time.
